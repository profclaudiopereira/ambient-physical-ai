# Context-Aware Cognitive Lab

## Official Demonstration Scenario

Ambient Physical AI will be demonstrated through a contextual laboratory environment inspired by the concepts of:

* Mark Weiser
* Ubiquitous Computing
* Context-Aware Computing
* Ambient Intelligence

The objective is to demonstrate how a distributed cognitive ecosystem can perceive, identify, understand and adapt to human presence.

---

# Scenario

The demonstration takes place inside a technology laboratory or innovation space.

The environment contains multiple specialized nodes connected through the Ambient Physical AI architecture.

The participant enters the laboratory and interacts naturally with the environment.

---

# Demonstration Flow

```text
Person enters
        ↓
Presence detected
        ↓
Identity recognized
        ↓
Context retrieved
        ↓
Cognitive reasoning
        ↓
Ambient adaptation
        ↓
Physical interaction
```

---

# Hardware Participation

## Presence

```text
Unit Mini ToF
```

Detects the arrival of a person.

---

## Identity

```text
M5Dial + NFC
```

Identifies the user and activates the contextual profile.

---

## Cognition

```text
AX630C + LLM Mate
```

Performs reasoning and contextual decision making.

---

## Ambient Runtime

```text
PoE-P4
```

Transforms the environment according to cognitive decisions.

---

## Expression

```text
StackChan
```

Provides embodiment and visual interaction.

---

## Voice

```text
Voice Pyramid + AtomS3R
```

Provides conversational interaction.

---

# Demonstration Example

Professor Claudio enters the laboratory.

The system detects presence through the ToF sensor.

The user is identified through NFC.

The AX630C retrieves contextual information.

The Ambient Runtime Node adapts the environment.

StackChan greets the user.

Voice services become available.

Environmental information is presented to the user.

The environment becomes contextually aware of who arrived and adapts its behavior accordingly.

---

# Key Message

Ambient Physical AI is not a home automation system.

It is a context-aware cognitive environment capable of perceiving, interpreting and adapting to human presence through a distributed architecture of specialized runtime nodes.
